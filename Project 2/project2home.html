About Angela Peng

Hi everyone!

My name is Angela Peng and I am a third year student studying with a double major in Communication, Culture, Information and Technology (CCIT) and Technology, Coding and Society (TCS) at University of Toronto Mississauga. I am currently interesting with studying design and artificial intelligence which are important to technologies today. I am very fluent in English, Mandarin, Shanghainese and French, and can also speak basic Spanish. My favourite colour is pink and all girls like pink. My other favourite colours are yellow, red, white, silver, beige and brown. Outside of design and artificial intelligence as my interest, my other interests include piano, listening to music, skating, drawing and painting.

I like to travel as well and have been to many cities such as Shanghai (my hometown), Guangzhou, Paris, Rome, Amsterdam, Montreal, Chicago, Washington DC, Newark, Ottawa, Shenzhen, Tokyo, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Beijing, Vienna, Munich, Edinburgh, Halifax, Detroit, New York, Salt Lake City, Las Vegas, London UK, and Budapest.
